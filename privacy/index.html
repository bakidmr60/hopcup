Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026 — Hop Cup is built with a strict on-device privacy design: zero ad networks, zero analytics SDKs, and zero developer servers. Even this support website uses zero cookies, trackers, or external scripts.

1. On-Device & Personal iCloud Progress

  • Cleared levels, earned stars, Daily Cup streaks, Blueprint Hint balance, audio/haptic preferences, and Toybox theme selections stay locally on your device via Apple UserDefaults.
  • When iCloud is enabled on your Apple Account, progress syncs privately across your own devices using Apple's NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore. The developer has no server access to your iCloud container or gameplay records.

2. StoreKit 2 In-App Purchases

  • Optional unlocks (Full Game Unlock, Complete Toybox Edition, Toybox Cosmetics Upgrade, and 10 Blueprint Hints) are processed directly by Apple through the App Store.
  • Purchase entitlements are cryptographically verified on-device via Apple StoreKit 2. We never receive or store your name, payment card, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

3. Zero Data Collection & Zero Tracking

  • Hop Cup declares NSPrivacyTracking = false and an empty NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes manifest in its Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy).
  • We collect zero personal identifiers, IDFA, device fingerprints, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, crash telemetry, or behavioral profiling data.

4. All-Ages Safety & Contact

  • Because Hop Cup collects zero personal information or device identifiers from any player and contains no third-party advertising or unmoderated chat, it is safe for players of all ages (App Store 4+).
  • Questions about privacy or support can be submitted anytime through our official Player Support Tracker.
