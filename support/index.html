Hop Cup Support & Help

Need help restoring purchases, syncing chapters across iPhone and iPad, or reporting a puzzle bug? We respond promptly to all player requests.

Open a Support Request

Restoring Purchases on a New iPhone or iPad

  • Open Hop Cup, tap the Toybox button (or open Workshop & Settings), and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the sheet while signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Non-consumable unlocks (Full Game Unlock, Complete Toybox Edition, and Toybox Cosmetics Upgrade) restore immediately at no charge.

iCloud Progress Sync & Offline Play

  • Hop Cup works 100% offline — including all 48 campaign puzzles and every Daily Cup puzzle.
  • When iCloud Drive / iCloud Key-Value sync is enabled in iOS Settings, cleared levels, stars, and hint counts sync automatically between your iPhone and iPad.

Billing & Refund Requests

  • Because all transactions are processed directly by Apple without developer access to billing records, refund requests can be submitted directly through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
